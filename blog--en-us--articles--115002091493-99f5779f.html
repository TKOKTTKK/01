How to download imToken safely?

Updated at 2026-11-12

Visit the official imToken website at: https://token.im

For Android Users

Click “Try imToken”, then select “Android APK” or “Google Play” to download the App.

For iOS Users

Click “App Store”- “GET” to install the App.

What should I do if the App Store shows “App Not Available”?

If you see “App Not Available” in the App Store, it means imToken isn’t available in your Apple ID’s region. 

If our website shows “imToken is currently available on all App Stores outside mainland China,” please switch to a non–mainland China Apple ID to download imToken.

Important: When downloading from the App Store, make sure to verify the App developer as IMTOKEN PTE.LTD. All other imToken Apps are counterfeit.

What should I do if I can’t access the imToken website?

If you can’t access the imToken website or the in-app update link on your current network, email [email protected] with the subject line “Download” to receive the official download links.

In the reply email, tap Download Android APK, Get it on Google Play, or Download on the App Store. You can also scan the QR code in the email to download or update imToken.

Note: If your email app or default browser blocks APK downloads, open the download link in another browser, such as Edge or Chrome.

PSA：

To protect your tokens, always download imToken from the official website: https://token.im. Using other methods can lead to counterfeit versions and potential theft.

Avoid unsafe methods such as:

  • Downloading via links, posters, QR codes or APKs shared by others.
  • Searching for "imToken download" on search engines like Google.
  • Downloading from third-party websites.
  • Searching for "imToken" directly on the App Store.

Scammers are constantly improving their tactics. Stay alert and double-check that you are visiting the official site to ensure you're downloading the genuine imToken App.
