Error “Invalid address” when sending tokens from exchange to wallet

Updated at 2026-11-13

You’ll get the error “Invalid address” if the wallet address you entered into the exchange does not match the withdrawal network you selected.

Before transferring tokens from an exchange to imToken, verify that imToken supports the token type and the withdrawal network. Sending an unsupported token will show a successful withdrawal from the exchange but the assets will not appear in your imToken wallet.
