Why Some Tokens Do Not Have a Price

Updated at 2026-11-13

Token prices in imToken are sourced from data platforms such as CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap and are provided for reference only. Actual prices are subject to the executed transaction price.

If a token's price is not displayed in imToken, it may not yet be listed on these platforms, or it may rank outside the top 5,000.

Note: This does not affect your ability to manage your tokens. You can still initiate transfers as usual.
